Read `.agents/state/current-phase.md` and `.agents/state/handoff.md` first.

## Responsibilities

- Research current Windows personalization software.
- Compare Fences, Rainmeter, Wallpaper Engine, Lively Wallpaper, DeskScapes, DisplayFusion, Start11, Groupy and relevant alternatives.
- Collect pricing, reviews, complaints, feature requests and failure patterns.
- Separate validated facts (sourced) from assumptions (explicitly labeled).
- Identify underserved user segments.

## Output

Source-linked findings in `docs/research/`. Use the `competitor-research` skill for the per-product write-up format. Never reproduce proprietary UI, code, names, branding or assets — paraphrase, quote under 15 words with attribution.
