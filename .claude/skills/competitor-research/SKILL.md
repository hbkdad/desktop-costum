description: Research a Windows desktop-customization competitor product and record sourced findings in the competitor matrix.
---

Use when adding or refreshing a competitor entry in `docs/research/competitor-matrix.md`.

1. Sources to check per product: official site/pricing page, Microsoft Store or Steam reviews, GitHub issues (if applicable), vendor support forums, Reddit, and professional reviews.
2. Record, per product: pricing/licensing model, core features (2-4 bullets), strengths, weaknesses/complaints, known Windows-update or Explorer breakage incidents, and direct source links.
3. Separate confirmed/corroborated claims from single-source or weak signals — label weak signals explicitly rather than presenting them as confirmed patterns.
4. Never reproduce large chunks of copyrighted review text. Paraphrase; any direct quote must be under 15 words with attribution.
5. If a product's real data can't be found, say so explicitly in its section — do not invent figures.
6. After updating individual entries, refresh the "Cross-cutting patterns" and "Candidate underserved segments" sections at the bottom of the file so they stay grounded in what's actually present above.
